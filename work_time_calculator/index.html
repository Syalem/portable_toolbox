◷ Work Time Calculator

Calculate when your workday ends, including the minimum break time.

How it works

  • You need 8 hours of actual work.
  • If no break is specified, a 30-minute break is included.
  • If a break is specified, the calculator adds extra break time as needed to reach at least 30 minutes total.
  • The result can roll over to the next day.
Break rule: specified break duration + enough extra time to reach a total of at least 30 minutes. A break longer than 30 minutes is counted as-is.
